What we fix on the spot

Most problems are solved roadside in minutes. For the rest, we tow you where you need to go.

  • Towing

    Safe transport to your home, your mechanic or the nearest shop.

  • Jump start

    Dead battery? We get you running again, or replace it on site.

  • Flat tire change

    We swap on your spare so you can get back on the road safely.

  • Lockout service

    Keys locked inside? We open the door without damaging your car.

  • Fuel delivery

    Ran dry? We bring enough fuel to reach the next station.

  • Winching & recovery

    Stuck in mud, snow or a ditch? We pull you back onto the road.

Back on the road in three steps

  1. 1

    1. Call us

    Tell us where you are and what happened. We'll confirm the details in under a minute.

  2. 2

    2. We dispatch

    The nearest Highway Hero is sent to your location with an arrival estimate.

  3. 3

    3. You drive on

    We fix it on the spot, or tow you to safety. Then you're on your way.

Where we cover
